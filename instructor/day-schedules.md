# Day schedules

Times are indicative for a 09:00 start with a one-hour lunch. Each day has about
eight hours of work including roughly an hour of reading per module; the facilitator
guide lists the steps to drop for a seven-hour day, and the Background sections can be
read the evening before. Breaks are not scheduled; participants take them at module
boundaries.

## Day 0 (before arrival, self-paced, 6 h)

| Block | Module |
|---|---|
| 0.5 h | m00 Setup |
| 1.5 h | m01 JAX warm-up |
| 1.0 h | m02 Diagnostic |
| 3.0 h | m02b Bayesian inference from first principles |
